# Product

## Register

brand

## Users
Recruiters, hiring managers, freelance clients, and other developers looking for mobile/AI expertise.

## Product Purpose
To showcase Andi Irham's portfolio and expertise in Android, Kotlin, and Flutter development, and to drive visitors to contact for work or hiring opportunities.

## Brand Personality
Professional, Technical, Minimalist.

## Anti-references
Overly loud or chaotic designs, "Web3" neon excesses, or generic un-styled SaaS templates. The design should avoid clutter and let the work speak for itself.

## Design Principles
- **Show, Don't Tell**: Let the projects and code examples demonstrate technical capability.
- **Expert Confidence**: A quiet, minimalist aesthetic that feels assured rather than loud.
- **Performance as a Feature**: The site must load fast and feel technically sound, reflecting developer expertise.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
Standard WCAG AA compliance. Ensure high contrast for readability and support keyboard navigation.
